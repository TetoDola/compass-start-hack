# Pitch Storyline — First Draft

## 1. The call

Meet a portfolio manager in the middle of an ordinary workday.

Then the phone rings. A stressed client is on the line:

> “What the fuck is happening? My portfolio is down 12%. Why? How exposed am I—and what should we do now?”

The portfolio manager feels the pressure immediately. The client expects a clear, credible answer now—not after hours of searching through holdings, research reports, market feeds, and news.

## 2. The problem

The information needed to explain the loss exists, but it is fragmented. The manager must determine:

- which positions are driving the decline;
- what companies, industries, and countries sit underneath those positions;
- which news and market events affect those exposures;
- how large the effect is; and
- what remains at risk.

Doing that manually is slow. In a volatile market, every minute without an answer weakens the client's confidence.

## 3. The product moment

The portfolio manager opens our platform and gets a live X-ray of the client's portfolio.

The platform shows every position and maps its underlying exposure across companies, industries, and countries. It connects relevant news and market events to those exposures, identifies what is driving the loss, and estimates the effect on the portfolio.

Instead of searching across disconnected systems, the manager can see the full chain in one place:

**Market event → affected exposure → impacted positions → portfolio effect**

## 4. The outcome

Within minutes, the portfolio manager can return to the client with a confident explanation:

- what changed;
- why the portfolio is down;
- which positions and exposures matter most;
- what could happen next; and
- which actions the client should consider.

The result is more than faster analysis. It helps the portfolio manager turn a moment of panic into a moment of trust.

## Core promise

**From “What the fuck is happening?” to a clear, actionable answer—in minutes.**
